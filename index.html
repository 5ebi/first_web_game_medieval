How to Play

Basics

  • Pick a building on the right, then click the map to place it.
  • Lumberjacks need a forest, quarries and mines need stone deposits within 5 tiles. The dashed box shows the search radius.
  • Every working villager eats 1 food per day. Idle villagers feed themselves.
  • Three days without food and your people leave. Game over.
  • Resources are capped by storage. Build the Warehouse early.
  • Click a placed building for details or to demolish it (50% refund).
  • Your progress is saved automatically.

Controls

  • Drag move the map
  • Wheel + - zoom
  • Arrows scroll the map
  • Space pause / resume
  • 1 2 3 game speed
  • Esc / Right click cancel
  • Q…G building hotkeys (shown in the menu)
  • Del demolish selected building
  • C center on the Castle
  • M mute   H this help
